feat(customer-app): improve booking confirmation and add to project flow

What changed:
- Added a new Booking Confirmation screen that appears right after you place any booking (labour, material, or equipment rental).
- This new screen gives you clear feedback that your order was successful and provides a direct "Add to Project" button.
- Updated the "Add to Project" screen so it can handle attaching multiple items at once. For example, if you buy materials from 3 different sellers in one checkout, you can now add all 3 orders to a project with a single click.
- Updated the underlying booking process so it passes the correct booking or order information to the new confirmation screen.

Why it matters:
- This provides a much smoother experience after completing a purchase, explicitly confirming success before taking you back to your bookings list.
- It saves you time by letting you group multiple material orders into a project instantly, rather than having to do it one by one later.
